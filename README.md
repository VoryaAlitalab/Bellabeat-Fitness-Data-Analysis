# Bellabeat-Fitness-Data-Analysis
Bellabeat Fitness Data Analysis 

The case study follows the six-step data analysis process introduced in the Google Data Analytics Certificate: Ask, Prepare, Process, Analyze, Share, and Act.

## Scenario:

Founded in 2013, Bellabeat has experienced rapid growth, establishing itself as a technology-driven wellness company focused on women's health. The company offers five main products: the Bellabeat app, Leaf, Time, Spring, and Bellabeat Membership.

Although Bellabeat is currently a successful small company, it has significant potential to expand its presence in the global smart device market. we have been tasked with analyzing smart device usage data to better understand how consumers interact with and use their devices. The insights gained from this analysis will help inform the company's marketing strategy and identify opportunities for future growth.
## 1-Ask
Business Task:

Analyse Fitbit fitness tracker data to understand how consumers use fitness tracking devices and identify usage patterns and trends. The findings will provide actionable insights to support and inform Bellabeat's marketing strategy.

Stakeholders:

Primary stakeholders: Urška Sršen and Sando Mur, members of Bellabeat's executive team.

Secondary stakeholders: Bellabeat's marketing analytics team.
## 2-Prepare
FitBit Fitness Tracker Data [https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arashnic/fitbit] : This Kaggle data set contains personal fitness tracker data from thirty fitbit users. Thirty eligible Fitbit users consented to the submission of personal tracker data, including minute-level output for physical activity, heart rate, and sleep monitoring. It includes information about daily activity, steps, and heart rate that can be used to explore users’ habits.
